✦ HOW IT WORKS

Three gentle steps, then it is gone

LetItGo Log is built to carry nothing. You say the thing, someone somewhere answers with warmth, and the whole moment quietly dissolves.

🪶
01

Drop it in

Write your stress, your worry, or just the shape of a bad day. Pick a mood if you like. There is no name field, because there is no you here — only the feeling.

🤍
02

Be held by strangers

Others read your note and send warmth or leave a gentle reply. You may pass a hundred people and never know, but the kindness is real and it is for you.

⏱
03

Watch it disappear

After 24 hours, every note and every comment fades away completely. Nothing is archived. Nothing follows you home. You are free to start again tomorrow.
